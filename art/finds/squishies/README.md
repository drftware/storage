# Squishies

Soft finds that squash, dent and spring back (`art/finds/WAVES.md`, F-05), built with the finds
kit (`art/finds/CRAFT.md`). `scripts/art.sh set squishies` bakes, exports and renders them;
`spec.py` holds every number, `build.py` the finds. Templates are in the place at
`ReplicatedStorage.Props.Finds.<Find>` once imported (`Finds/Kit.luau`).

## Squishy butter stick (F-05a, Nov 16)

**References.** Slow-rise butter squishies sold in 2026: a 5.51 x 1.57 x 1.57 in listing
(walmart.com/ip/19931208013), a 5.5 in one held and bent in the hand
(agrifeedpetsupply.com/products/butter-squishy-toy-5-5), a 6 in one printed with its weight and
the word butter in navy (flyingpigtoys.com, "Fun Doh 6 in Butter Stick"), others at 5.1 and
5.25 in. All are sunny yellow PU foam blocks with well-rounded edges and the wrapper printed
straight onto the foam. A real wrapper's tablespoon marks (a stick is eight tablespoons) are its
other tell.

**Size.** 140 x 40 x 40 mm (5.5 x 1.57 x 1.57 in): 0.55 x 0.157 x 0.157 studs. Every edge
rounded to 7 mm.

**Tells.** The butter-yellow block; BUTTER in bold navy on its front; tablespoon ticks over its
top. From a unit's doorway it is a yellow speck (a small find); in the hand it reads at once.

**What it is.** One part, `Stick`, lofted in two halves from each end to the middle so its top
has points close together where a thumb goes (3,570 triangles). The print is ink drawn in code
(`bu_ink`: a stroke font for the word, the ticks and their little labels) and sampled by the
look by position. Foam: matt with fine pores and a few pits, the pour's faint mottling.

**Squeeze** (8.6 s + 0.4): a thumb's dent 8.5 mm deep and 15 mm across pressed into the top's
right half in a third of a second, held, let go (a little give back), then creeping flat over
about seven seconds, quickest first. Seven bones carry it (`bu_bones`, D-246): `Dent` sinks the
floor; `Rim1` to `Rim6`, half way out, sink half as far and tip 36 degrees toward the middle so
the dent's sides catch the light in Roblox. The ticks over the top dip into it.

**In Studio.** Uploaded (91493472138143) and imported with its eight bones (the root and seven);
the dent posed from a script shows in Edit (docs/STUDIO.md "Bones").

## Stress ball with a face (F-05b, Nov 16)

**References.** Rubber eye-popper squeeze balls: a promo smiley ball 1.75 in across, glossy blue
vinyl with two big white bubble eyes standing proud of the face and a thin printed black grin
(qualitylogoproducts.com/stress-balls/smilin-eye-poppin-pal.htm); a popping-eye squeeze toy with
clear bubble eyes (target.com, Toy Planet Pop Eye Squeeze Toy, A-94979556); listings of sensory
popping-eye balls at 2.76 in. Squeezed, the air inside shoves the eyes out of the face; let go,
they pop back in.

**Size.** 70 mm across (2.76 in): 0.276 studs, sitting 1.2 mm onto a small, soft flat spot.

**Tells.** The lime-green ball; two big white eyes with crossed black pupils; the grin. From a
unit's doorway it is a green speck (a small find); held, the face reads at once.

**What it is.** `Body` (the root): a lathed ball (its face in the middle of a texture strip), a
soft rubber lip round each socket, the mould's faint seam over its sides and top, a fill-plug
ring under it. `EyeL` and `EyeR`: white rubber bubbles (most of a sphere and the neck hidden in
the ball), each pivoting on its middle, its axis straight out of its socket, baked `alone` so
the ball's dark inside never shades them. The grin is ink drawn in code on an equidistant map
of the face (`sb_face_ink`), so its stroke is even all round the curve; the pupils are drawn by
direction from each eye's middle, looking 17 degrees in and 9 up. 1024 texture at 1520 texels a
stud; 5,072 triangles.

**Squeeze** (1.22 s + 0.3): the fingers close (it bulges up a hair), it squashes to 0.68 tall in
a quarter second, and a beat behind it each eye goes 7.6 mm out along its axis and swells 1.3
times, the right a hair after the left; held; let go, the ball springs back past round and
wobbles still while the eyes snap home a hair past and bounce once. Each eye stretches against
the ball's squash (D-247) so it stays round.

**In Studio.** Uploaded (87393666160557) and imported with both eyes on Motor6Ds; posed at the
squeeze's peak from a script, the ball's size went to 0.68 tall and 1.21 wide and each eye's to
about 1.27 times, round (D-247). Its positions move only in Play (Motor6D offsets).

## Mochi bun (F-05b2, Nov 16)

**References.** Powdered daifuku on Wikimedia Commons ("Assortment of mochis from the Niji store
(in Barcelona)", photos 1 to 4): soft domes slumped onto the plate, the side rolling under to a
smaller footprint, dusted with white starch in thick, crazed patches over the top and thin wisps
down the sides with the colour showing through. Mochi and bun squishies sold in 2026: 2 in minis
in TPR (target.com, A-93276370), a 5 x 1 in jumbo custard bun (walmart.com/ip/17278610743),
Puka's large dim-sum mystery buns (bannershallmark.com, squishy-dumplings); real ones are dusted
with powder to stop the TPR sticking.

**Size.** 72 mm across, 42 mm tall (2.8 x 1.65 in): 0.288 x 0.165 studs, a hair oval, its top
slumped 1.5 mm aside and 1 mm back.

**Tells.** The soft pink dome; the patchy white starch; a tiny face of our own (two dark oval
eyes, a small smile, rose blush) printed low on its front. From a unit's doorway it is a pale
speck (a small find); held, the face and the powder read at once.

**What it is.** `Body`, one lathed part: a flat foot, the side rolling under in a quarter circle
to its widest point 13 mm up, then a superellipse dome. The starch is drawn by thickness (facing
up, blotchy noise, streaks down the sides, thinner over the face where thumbs rub it): thin
frosting, thick matte patches, loose grains at their edges, and fine crazing where the thick
powder dried (two noise iso-lines; a Voronoi read as tiles). The face and blush are ink drawn
in code, seen straight on from the front (`mb_face_ink`). 1024 texture at 1798 texels a stud;
2,160 triangles.

**Squeeze** (3.2 s + 0.3): no spring at all. It barely gives as the fingers meet it, then sinks
heavy to 0.6 tall in under half a second, spreading wide; held, it creeps a little flatter; let
go, it heaves most of the way back in half a second and settles into shape over the next one and
a half, never past round. The game's soft puff of air goes at the sink.

**In Studio.** Uploaded (75693422662417) and imported; its size matched the Luau. Judged under a
0.6, range 11 bulb from a hand's distance: the face, blush and starch patches read, the pink a
touch peach in the warm light. Its front faces the template's LookVector (-Z).

## Squishy toast slice (F-05b3, Nov 16)

**References.** Toast-slice squishies sold in 2026, seen in an image search for "toast slice
squishy slow rise face" and "slow rising toast squishy in hand crust" (bing.com/images): PU foam
slices with a square base under a wider rounded crown that overhangs the sides at the shoulders,
a golden-brown crust airbrushed round the edge and darker over the crown, a pale cream crumb
toasted a touch golden next to the crust, many with a small kawaii face printed low on the front.
Sizes: a 4.3 in sticky bread squishy (Amazon, SUISUILE), a 5.4 x 5.4 x 1.9 in toast squishy
(fufusquishy.com), 5.1 in bread squishies (walmart.com/ip/19393364882); a real Pullman slice is
about 4 x 4 in.

**Size.** 104.8 mm across the crown, 92 mm across the base, 110.4 mm tall, 28 mm thick (4.1 x 4.3
x 1.1 in): 0.412 x 0.435 x 0.110 studs, standing on its bottom crust.

**Tells.** The slice's outline (the crown wider than the base, its gentle dip between two
shoulders); the brown crust ringing a pale face; our own face: two dark oval eyes with a white
shine, a little open smile with a pink tongue, soft coral blush. From a unit's doorway it is a
small pale speck with a brown rim; held, the slice and the face read at once.

**What it is.** `Body`, one part lofted from each face (a capped fan) round a 5.5 mm rounded
edge to the middle, the back half the front turned half a turn about Z. Its outline is a closed
Catmull-Rom curve through the spec's half outline and its mirror (`ts_outline`), its points
spaced by length plus turning so the corners get more. The crust, the toasted glow and the ragged
edge between them are drawn from how far each point lies inside the outline, a numpy image
projected along Y (`ts_crust_dist`), roughened by noise; the band is 5 mm on the sides and 8.5 mm
over the crown. The crumb's pores are three sizes of noise blobs stretched up and down as bread
rises, sunk in the bump with their lower lips lighter. The face is ink drawn in code
(`ts_face_ink`). 1024 texture at 1278 texels a stud; 2,112 triangles. Judged on quick sheets
twice (fixed: a cooler, flatter crumb, faint pores, a smooth crust, small eyes, the filmstrip
clipping the crown), full sheets once, and in Studio under a warm 0.6 bulb (template in
`ReplicatedStorage.Props.Finds.ToastSlice`, uploaded 109999579926471).

**Squeeze** (1.26 s + 0.3): springs back like bread. The fingers meet it and it bulges a hair,
then crushes fast to 0.75 tall under the thumb; held a moment; let go, it springs back to 1.05 in
a seventh of a second, dips to 0.97 and settles inside two thirds of a second, softer than the
rubber ball's ring. Whole-body `stretch` (`K.SPRING`).

## Jumbo slow-rise strawberry (F-05c, Nov 16)

**References.** Jumbo strawberry squishies sold in 2026, seen in an image search for "jumbo slow
rise strawberry squishy" (bing.com/images) and four listing photos opened from it: an 8 in giant
vinyl strawberry with sculpted seed pits (m.media-amazon.com/images/I/616-nbX4-tL), a classic
slow-rise pair with a star cap and a stem (walmart.com, "Giant Strawberry Squishy, Classic
Slow-Rise"), a 25 cm one held in two hands (banggood), and a PU foam jumbo printed with yellow
teardrop seeds (m.media-amazon.com/images/I/51FdZzlfavL). Sizes: Sunny Days' Squeezy Strawberry 5
x 5 x 5 in (target.com A-94757072), a Walmart "extra large" 5 in, a half jumbo 13 cm tall
(lmk.today). A fat rounded cone widest a little over half way up, rich red, a flat green cap of
pointed leaves lying over the shoulder round a short stem.

**Size.** 112 mm across, 121 mm to the top of the shoulder, 136 mm to the stem's tip (4.4 x 4.8
in, 5.4 in with the stem): 0.45 x 0.54 x 0.44 studs, standing on the little flat spot a soft
foam tip settles onto.

**Tells.** The strawberry shape (the rounded cone, the tip it stands on); rows of yellow seeds
on red; the green star of a cap and its stem. From a unit's doorway it is a red ball with a green
top; held, the seeds and the leaves read at once.

**What it is.** `Berry`, one part (the cap is in the same mesh: Roblox's importer turns an FBX
with bones and two meshes into a Motor6D rig and drops the bones). The berry is lofted ring by
ring up a Catmull-Rom outline (`sr_curve`), three broad lobes round it, a hair oval, its top
slumped a little aside; its points crowd at the front where the thumb goes (`sr_thetas`) and its
rings through the dent's heights (`sr_ring_s`). Each of six leaves (long and short in turn, a
little uneven) is lofted along the skin from under the hub to its point, every ring's points
slid round the berry so it hugs it across, lifting toward the tip, a lens in section with a
groove down its middle; a low hub and a leaning stem on top. The seeds are an unrolled numpy
image (`sr_seed_ink`): staggered rows, each as many seeds as fit round it, every teardrop drawn
at its true size where it lands, sitting in a round pit sunk in the bump. Airbrushed deeper red
toward the tip and lighter, warmer over the shoulder. 1024 texture at 888 texels a stud; 10,286
triangles (dense where the dent bends). Judged on quick sheets twice (fixed: a thin, small cap
that didn't read in the hand, an apple-round tip, tan seeds, the filmstrip filming the dent
edge-on), full sheets once, and in Studio under a warm 0.6 bulb, the dent pressed by
`Finds.pose` (template in `ReplicatedStorage.Props.Finds.JumboStrawberry`, uploaded
81603530188772).

**Squeeze** (11.1 s + 0.5): the dent takes ten seconds to rise back. A thumb goes 12 mm into its
front in 0.38 s, slowing as the foam fights it; held; let go, it gives a little back at once,
then rises slowly, quickest first, and creeps the last of the way smooth ten seconds after the
thumb let go (`K.RISE`). The butter stick's bones on a curved side (`sr_bones`): a middle bone
sliding along the skin's inward normal and eight round it, half way out, sliding half as far and
tipping their share of the bowl's slope toward the middle, so its sides catch the light.
